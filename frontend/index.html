Why FoodRescue Lite?

Every meal saved is a step toward a zero-waste community.

1. Reduce Food Waste

Prevent safe, perfectly edible surplus batches from canteens and events from ending up in landfills.

2. Share Surplus Food

Quickly post available food with quantity, pickup location, and availability deadline in under 60 seconds.

3. Help Your Community

Volunteers and coordinators can claim food and transport it to shelters, hostels, or community centers.
